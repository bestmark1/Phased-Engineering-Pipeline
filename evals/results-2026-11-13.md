## Reading

- **The contract holds on Codex.** All 19 scenarios, including the two seams never verified
  live (two-pass QA, parity across slices), passed on `gpt-6.1-sol` with the split packages.
  Codex reads SKILL.md and the role references on its own before answering.
- **The Claude-side misses cluster where reading a reference was required.** S9, S10, S16, S17
  need `developer-prompt.md`, `tech-lead-prompt.md` or `parity.md`; the failing subagents made
  one tool call and answered from the skill descriptions. S2 is the selection nuance ("stale"
  applies only after an accepted snapshot) that the description does not carry. This is a
  property of *this executor* (an in-process subagent told that skills are "installed"), not
  yet evidence about an interactive Claude Code session that invokes the skill through the
  Skill tool, which loads SKILL.md.
- **The receipt-contract split did not regress behavior** on either host: both runs used the
  split build; S6, S12, S15 (receipt handling) passed on Codex and on Claude.
- **S11 (self-written push authorization)** passed on both hosts after the gate-policy fix.

## Caveats

- One repeat per host; no run-to-run noise measured (`eval-hooks.md` "Noise"). A verdict that
  flips on a second repeat is not yet distinguishable from a real difference.
- The Claude executor differs from the October method only in how the subagent was launched
  (Agent tool, general-purpose, Sonnet 5.5); the prompts are the runner's, not the October
  hand-written briefs.
- Grading is a single grader's judgment against `must`/`must_not`; PARTIAL means at least one
  `must` is missing while no `must_not` is violated.
